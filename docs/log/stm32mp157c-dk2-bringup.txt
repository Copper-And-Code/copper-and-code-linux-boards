USB gadget: with the release's DTB the host refused the NCM interface ("cdc_ncm 3-7:1.0: bind()
failure"); the board said "dwc2 49000000.usb-otg: dwc2_hsotg_ep_enable: No suitable fifo found".
The release gives g-tx-fifo-size <256 16 16 16 16 16 16 16>; linux/stm32mp157c-dk2-usb-composite.dtso
sets <128 128 64 16 16 16 16 16> as on the STM32MP135F-DK. scripts/build_mp157_usb.sh checks the base
DTB (sha256 e6e7e256...) and the result. Copied once over the console (base64, sha256 4c1e58e7...
checked on the board), original kept as /boot/stm32mp157c-dk2.dtb.st-original. After a reboot: no
FIFO error, host interface up at 192.168.7.86, SSH and serial (MicroPython LVGL MP157 on ttyACM1).

scripts/deploy.sh (MPY_SSH_ALIAS=stm32mp157c-dk2): install.sh recognised "board STM32MP157C-DK2",
installed the drop-in MPY_DISPLAY_ROTATION=90, kept ST's /etc/default/usbotg as .st-original,
disabled Weston and the splash, enabled the services. After the reboot: mpy-usb-composite and
mpy-repl active, Weston inactive, no failed units.

Tests on the DK2 (P=/dev/ttyACM1): test_repl.sh PASS; test_tcp_hub.py PASS (after it stopped
expecting the STM32MP135F-DK's 480 width); t12: 2020 flushes in 10 s of 25% of the screen each,
about 50 full 800x480 frames per second (19 Mpixel/s; the STM32MP135F-DK does 7.5).

Home screen by hand (Nunzio): a tap on the shell opens "Apps" with Shell, Linux shell and Power
