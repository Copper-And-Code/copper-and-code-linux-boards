machine.I2C and machine.ADC on the Cortex-M4 (2026-11-18):
  Device tree: SDK's stm32mp157c-dk2-m4-examples.dtb (sha256 5d0188f1...) + our USB FIFO overlay,
  installed as /boot/stm32mp157c-dk2.dtb (the one before kept as .usb-before-m4). Compared with the
  base DTB, Linux loses only adc@48003000 and dma-controller@48001000; the M4 gets TIM2, I2C5, DAC,
  UART7, TIM1, SPI4, DMA2, ADC, HASH, RNG, CRC, CRYP, the button and the orange LED (PH7).
  Reboot: compatible "st,stm32mp157c-dk2-m4-examples", USB gadget, REPL and sound cards unchanged.
  Pins: Linux marks PA11/PA12 "reserved" for the M4 but leaves them analog (pinconf): the firmware
  sets them (AF4 open drain, weak pull-ups). Clocks: i2c5_k 64 MHz (HSI), adc12_k 24 MHz (ck_per);
  vref regulator (VREFBUF) 2500 mV, enabled for the M4 by rproc-srm.
  Arduino analog pins from the schematic net names in UM2534 (dm00591354.pdf): A0 ANA0 ADC1_IN0,
  A1 ANA1 ADC1_IN1, A2 PF14 ADC2_IN6, A3 PF13 ADC2_IN2, A4 PC3 ADC1_IN13, A5 PF12 ADC1_IN6 (the
  manual's Table 23 has shifted rows). PA4, PA5, PC3, PF12, PF13, PF14 UNCLAIMED by Linux.
  Problems met:
    - I2C answered ETIMEDOUT and scanned in 3 ms: pins not set (see above). Then ENODEV, as wanted.
    - ADC internal channels refused (HAL_ADC_ConfigChannel): ADC1 was on (CR 0x1fc00101, ADEN)
      before the M4 started, left by the boot loader (USB-C power detection on ADC1). Turning it
      off was not enough (ADC1 then never finished a conversion); a reset of the ADC12 block
      through RCC at first use fixed both.
    - /dev/ttyRPMSG0 became /dev/ttyRPMSG1 after an M4 restart: mpy_m4 looks the port up.
  Results (tests/hil/test_m4.sh, nothing wired):
    firmware "mpy-m4 stm32mp157c-dk2", protocol 1; I2C5 100 kHz and 400 kHz; empty bus scanned in
    3 ms (400 kHz), 12 ms (100 kHz); ENODEV on writeto, readfrom, readfrom_mem, writeto_mem and
    the register read with a repeated start; VREFINT 1.203..1.216 V (OTP factory value 24017/65535
    of 3.3 V = 1.209 V); CORE_TEMP 0.647 V (calibration values at OTP 0x5c/0x5e give 43 C against
    29.9 C from the thermal zone: not used); A0..A5 floating, drifting to 0; 341 us a reading
    (2872 a second); LED and machine.Pin beside the M4.
  Cold boot: M4 offline; the first machine.I2C(5) starts mpy_m4.elf in 613 ms.
  Regression: test_repl.sh, test_tcp_hub.py, test_touch_evdev.py pass; the shell is on the screen.
