machine.SPI on SPI4 (2026-11-18):
  Pins from the m4-examples device tree: SCK PE12 (D13), MISO PE13 (D12), MOSI PE14 (D11), AF5;
  PE11 (D10, NSS) UNCLAIMED by Linux: chip select through machine.Pin on Linux. spi4_k 64 MHz.
  First transfer: ETIMEDOUT. Registers: CR1 0x1000 (SPE off), SR 0x10200 (MODF): MASTER was set
  while SSI was 0, the internal chip select looked active and the mode fault turned SPI off.
  Fixed: SSI before MASTER, all flags cleared (IFCR) before each transfer.
  No device at hand: during a transfer the M4 samples PE12/PE14 and rebuilds the MOSI bytes at
  the sampling edges. 8 modes (CPOL, CPHA, MSB/LSB) at 250 kHz: a53c0180ff005a96 seen as sent,

  4 MHz and above it does not (22, 6, 2 edges seen). MISO: ffffffff with the pull-up, 00000000
  with the pull-down. 250 kHz..32 MHz (asked 100 MHz -> 32 MHz). 1024 bytes at 32 MHz in 1399 us
  (732 kB/s); 2-byte write 363 us. test_m4.sh, test_repl.sh, test_tcp_hub.py pass.

machine.Timer and events from the M4 (2026-11-18):
  Facts: MicroPython with threads and GIL; LVGL in display_driver's own thread; micropython.schedule
  present. Chosen (B): events on the same RPMsg port (sequence 0), a reader thread in mpy_m4,
  handlers outside the port's lock. Protocol version 2.
  M4: 8 software timers on DWT->CYCCNT extended to 64 bits, deadlines in cycles, every expiry
  counted however late the loop is.
  First version: 10 ms for 1 s -> 100 calls, gaps median 9993 us (5274..14956); one shot 50 ms
  -> 50810 us; ADC requests inside callbacks while the main thread makes its own. 1 kHz with
  the main thread in a pure Python loop: the right count, but all 1138 callbacks within 33 ms at
  the end: the unix port's GIL is not fair, the reader thread waited, and the events piled up in
  the tty (they would overflow its 64 KB after about 5 s).
  Fixed with acknowledgements: after its handlers ran, Python sends OP_EVENT_ACK; until then the
  M4 sends no further event for that timer and counts the expiries into the next (timer.missed).
  1 kHz idle: 986 calls + 14 missed = 1000; busy 1 s: 8 calls + 872 missed, nothing piled up.
  test_m4.sh (twice), test_repl.sh, test_tcp_hub.py pass. PWM at 100 kHz measured 29.71% once
  for 30%: 80 samples a period, an interrupt shows; tolerance 0.5% there.

ADC.read_timed: sampling at a fixed rate on the M4 (2026-11-18):
  TIM2 (tim2_k 208877930 Hz, 32 bits, TRGO on update) triggers the ADC (EXTSEL TIM2_TRGO); the
  end-of-conversion interrupt (priority 0) stores into a 32768-sample buffer (64 KB of the M4's
  128 KB m_data; 20 KB used before). Sampling time: the longest within 70% of the period.
  VREFINT, 2000 samples: 1 kHz -> 1000.00 Hz, 10 kHz -> 9999.90 Hz, 100 kHz -> 99989.58 Hz
  (first-to-last conversion on DWT), mean 1.2063 V, no overruns; 32768 at 100 kHz: no overruns.
  Signal: Linux drove PF14 (A2) as a GPIO output while the M4 sampled it: the ADC saw nothing
  (0.05 V), while mpy_m4.measure saw 50.00 Hz 49.9% digitally: the ADC needs the pin analog.
  Instead Linux charges the pin (output high), the capture makes it analog: 1.85..1.89 V then
  x0.87 a sample, the same at 10, 50 and 100 kHz (charge taken by the sampling capacitor), faster
  at 1 kHz (leakage over 1 ms adds).
  machine.ADC.read_timed: 32768 at 100 kHz in 408 ms with the collection (742 ms once inside the
  test, after the other tests: collection is Python-bound); a 10 ms timer kept running (106 calls
  in a 1 s capture); bytearray gives the top 8 bits (123 for 1.206 V). test_m4.sh twice,
  test_repl.sh, test_tcp_hub.py pass.

Logic analyser (2026-11-18):
  OP_PORT_CAPTURE: GPIOx->IDR sampled on DWT->CYCCNT deadlines (period = core / rate, integer: 1 MHz
  asked gives 1.0042 MHz), into the capture buffer; up to 4 MHz, a second at most.
  UART "Hello" written before the capture: gone before the capture began (a request takes 0.3 ms).
  Started by the capture through the ring and the UART interrupt: first characters garbled, 3 late
  samples (the interrupt filling the FIFO at time zero). Fed by the sampling loop instead: still
  garbled the same way at 9600, 115200 and 1 Mbaud: the line had no idle before the first start
  bit, the decoder locked on a data edge. The buses now start after the first 5% of the capture:
  "Hello, M4!" whole at all three rates, SPI A5 3C with 84 samples of idle first.
  On the board (the example): UART decoded and drawn; SPI MOSI A5 3C 0F, MISO FF, CS low; PWM
  10000.1 Hz 30.0%. test_m4.sh (with the new check) and test_repl.sh pass.
