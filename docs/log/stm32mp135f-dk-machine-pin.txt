machine.Pin on STM32MP135F-DK, 2026-11-17

Source for the LEDs: UM2993 Rev 2, table 11. Blue LD3 on PA14 and red LD4 on PA13, active low
(the same lines as B1 and B2, table 12); green LD7 on MCP23017 IO14 and orange LD6 on IO15,
active high. On the board the MCP23017 is gpiochip9 (label "mcp23017"), lines 14 and 15 unused.

tests/hil/t17_mp135_pin.py (inputs only): MP135_PIN_TEST_PASS
  Pin('PA4', mode=IN, pull=PULL_UP); pull-up -> 1, pull-down -> 0 (floating)
  PA14 (gpio-keys) -> OSError EBUSY; same line twice -> EBUSY; value() after deinit() -> EBADF
  Pin(('mcp23017', 14)) -> Pin('gpiochip9:14', mode=IN, pull=None)
  Pin(('nochip', 1)) -> ValueError unknown GPIO chip nochip

tests/hil/t19_mp135_led.py (outputs, by eye):
  Pin('gpiochip9:14', mode=OUT) Pin('gpiochip9:15', mode=OUT)
  Nunzio confirmed: green LD7 blinked 5 times, then orange LD6 blinked 5 times, then both off.

tests/hil/t18_mp135_pin_loopback.py, jumper across CN8 pins 38 (PA5, output) and 40 (PA0, input),
UM2993 Rev 2 table 18: MP135_PIN_LOOPBACK_TEST_PASS
  push-pull 1/0/1/0, toggle, off; open drain: low driven, high released (follows the input's pull)
  After deinit(), gpioinfo shows PA0 input and PA5 output: the kernel keeps a released line's last
  configuration (here open drain, high, so not driven).
