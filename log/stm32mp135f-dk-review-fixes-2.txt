STM32MP135F-DK review fixes, second round, 2026-11-17

linuxproc: a background process that ignores SIGHUP kept the pseudo-terminal open after the
child exited, and run() looped without waiting until that process ended.
  Before (previous binary), tests/hil/t13 with "(trap '' HUP; exec sleep 5) & ...; exit 3":
    AssertionError: 5033   (run() returned after 5033 ms)
  After: MP135_LINUXPROC_TEST_PASS (returns at once with status 3 and the child's output)
  A plain "sleep 5 &" does not reproduce it: the shell's exit sends SIGHUP to its process group.

linuxfb.blit(): bounds checked against the panel before any product, buffer size in 64 bits.
  tests/hil/t16_mp135_blit_bounds.py: MP135_BLIT_BOUNDS_TEST_PASS (9 rectangles rejected,
  including 0x7FFFFFFF sizes and a stride chosen to overflow 32 bits; 2 valid edge cases accepted)

  Checked by hand with two fingers on the panel: see the end of this file.
